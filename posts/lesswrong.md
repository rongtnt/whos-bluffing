Title: HowSure: a daily calibration game, with a pre-registered test of whether feedback improves calibration

Short version: five 90%-interval questions a day, immediate feedback with sources, share grid, streaks, Slack app and classroom mode. Everything anonymous and open.

The research question is the one Lichtenstein & Fischhoff asked in 1980 with a few dozen subjects: does calibration training work? A daily game produces within-person data at a scale labs cannot. Pre-registration (including a survivorship check: are persistent players simply those who were well-calibrated on day one?) is in the repo; H3 is reported whichever way it comes out.

Prior art: Quantified Intuitions' Estimation Game (monthly, Fermi, teams) and Calibration Training; Open Philanthropy's Calibrate Your Judgment. HowSure is the daily, 30-second, shareable variant with open data.
