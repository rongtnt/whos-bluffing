Title: HowSure: a daily calibration game with a proper scoring rule, challenge links, and a pre-registered practice-effect study

Short version: 10 two-alternative comparison questions, confidence staked at 50–100%, scored with the quadratic rule (100 − 400(c − y)²), so honest reports maximize expected points. Ranked round daily, unlimited quick rounds, challenge links, Slack and Discord bots that post one question a day with a reveal. Everything anonymous; data released under CC BY-NC, code published to read.

The research question is Lichtenstein & Fischhoff's from 1980: does calibration training work? A game people return to produces within-person data at a scale labs cannot. The pre-registration (in the repo) includes a survivorship check: are persistent players simply those who were calibrated on day one? H3 is reported whichever way it comes out.

Prior art: Quantified Intuitions' Estimation Game and Calibration Training; Open Philanthropy's Calibrate Your Judgment. HowSure is the 30-second, social, proper-scoring-rule variant with open data.
