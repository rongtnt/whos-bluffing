# Slack App Directory listing (submit after the app has run in a few workspaces)

Short description (≤ 140 chars): One question a day that shows your team who's bluffing. Pick A or B, stake your confidence, see the reveal. No accounts.

Long description:
Who's Bluffing posts one comparison question to a channel every day ("Which is longer: the Nile or the Danube?"). Members tap A or B and stake how sure they are, from 50% to 100%. At the reveal, the channel sees the answer with its source, how the team split, the top scorers, and the day's biggest bluff. Honest confidence scores best; 100% wrong costs three times what 100% right earns.

- One install per workspace; the question posts at the hour you choose; the reveal follows a few hours later.
- Answers are private until the reveal; the leaderboard is public to the channel. Roast mode (naming the bluffer) is off unless you turn it on.
- Nothing stored but hashed member ids, answers and scores. No message text, no emails. Source published for transparency.
- Part of a pre-registered study of overconfidence; anonymous pooled data is released under CC BY-NC.
