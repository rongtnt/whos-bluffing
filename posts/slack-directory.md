# Slack App Directory listing (submit after the app has run in a few workspaces)

Short description (≤ 140 chars): A 30-second daily game that shows your team how overconfident it is. Five ranges, one leaderboard, no accounts.

Long description:
HowSure posts five questions to a channel every day. For each one, you give a range you're 90% sure contains the answer. Then everyone sees the truth, who caught it, and the team leaderboard. Most people get 2 or 3 of 5 — at "90% sure" — and that gap is the whole point: teams that know how sure they really are make better calls.

- One install per workspace; the game posts at the hour you choose.
- Play in a modal in under a minute; results are private, the leaderboard is public to the channel.
- Nothing stored but hashed member ids and scores. No message text, no emails. Open source.
- Part of an open study of overconfidence; anonymous pooled data is published.
