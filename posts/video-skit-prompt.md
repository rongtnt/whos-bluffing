## Scene 1 (12 s)

music. A startup office kitchen. Three AI-lab engineers lean on the counter: GREEN, a man in his late twenties in a
plain forest-green hoodie, holding up a phone; CLAY, a woman in her early thirties in a plain terracotta-orange hoodie;
BLACK, a man in his thirties in a plain black hoodie and black cap, holding a coffee. Fast, natural, lip-synced English

ninety percent sure." BLACK, wheezing: "That's minus three hundred, buddy." All three laugh; GREEN shakes his head.
