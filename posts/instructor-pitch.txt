EN (forwardable):
A free tool for the classic overconfidence demo: students take a 5-minute test on their phones (two-choice questions with confidence ratings + 90% ranges), and the class's calibration curve appears live on your screen. Create a class code at {URL}/class — no accounts, aggregates only, nothing shown until 5 students finish. English and Chinese. Open source and non-commercial.
