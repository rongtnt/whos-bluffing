# Outreach to AI leaders: the psychology, then the copy (2026-10-05)

You will contact these people yourself (follow, reply, DM where open). This file gives you the levers and ready
lines. Rules that keep it working: one reply per person per post, at most one DM, never under a somber post, every
fact true and sourced, polite throughout. Nothing here claims a number we do not have; `{n}` placeholders are filled
only from the live site when the threshold is met (100 or more players that day).

## Why a public figure plays a quiz (the six levers, strongest first)

1. **Competence threat, aimed at identity, not at them.** "Ten questions about your own company" is a dare they can
   answer in a minute; declining is a small public admission. Keep it about the field ("your own field"), never about
   the person's intelligence.
2. **Rivalry.** People defend a group faster than they defend themselves. "The questions are about OpenAI, Anthropic,
   xAI and Google" turns a quiz into a scoreboard. (A "claim your lab" leaderboard would make this lever ten times
   stronger; see the end.)
3. **Curiosity gap.** Name one specific question without the answer. The brain wants the gap closed, and closing it
   costs one tap.
4. **Foot in the door.** Ask one question inside the reply. Answering a reply is cheap; the link to the other nine
   follows naturally.
5. **Reciprocity and credit.** Offer something first: their company's facts quoted with sources, or the room's
   numbers sent to them after the day closes.
6. **Public commitment.** Post your own score first. Humility invites a match; bragging invites silence.

What never works on this audience: flattery, begging, "please check out", hashtags, emoji walls, and anything that
reads as a bot. Links in replies rank lower on X, so the reply carries the question and the link sits at the end,
short.

## If they answer

- They post a score: reply with thanks and one true line about the room ("{n} played today; the room was surest and
  wrong on {question}"), nothing else. Quote-post it from the account.
- They dispute a fact: reply with the source link; if they are right, fix the item and say so publicly the same day.
- They mock it: do not argue. "Fair. The data will tell." Then leave it.
