# Outreach to members of Congress (2026-10-05)

Same rules as the AI leaders file: one reply per person per post, at most one DM, never under a somber post, every fact true, polite, and nothing partisan in our words. The dare pages fill when the Politics item bank lands; until then the links return 404, so post nothing before the board shows the person.

Levers that fit this audience: supporters want to show they know their side; the party board (/parties) turns that into a score; the dare page keeps score of the member's silence. Never say a member is wrong about anything; never take a side.

## Party lines (post from the account, not as replies)

- `Which side knows its own record? Ten questions about Congress, scored on how sure you are. The board is public: whosbluffing.com/parties`
- `Democrats and Republicans are both on the board now. The averages appear at ten players a side. whosbluffing.com/parties`
