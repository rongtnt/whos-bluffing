# Reply templates for big AI threads on X

For replies under large accounts when they post about AI. Twelve replies, each built on one two-choice question with a
sourced answer, plus three quote posts for confident predictions. They're written in plain, complete sentences, the
way a thoughtful person writes on X: change words freely, but keep the thread address and the facts as they are. Every template is
at most 240 characters as X counts them (the link counts as 23). The answer and its sources sit under each template so
you can back it up if asked. The close calls (R1, R2, R4, R7, R8, R9) were checked against Wikipedia on 2026-10-04; for
the others, open the source before you use the template. No AI pack is live on the site yet, so the link leads to the
general game: the templates dare people to test their confidence there and never promise the answer is on the page.
Keep it that way.

## When to use

- Reply within 10 minutes of their post, or skip it.
- One reply per post. Never twice in the same thread. Answering someone who replies to you is fine; a second template is not.
- Stop a template after 3 uses (tick the boxes under it).
- Match the topic: a parameter question under a post about model size, a history question under a post about where things are heading.
- Each reply speaks to the thread ("most people in this thread", "anyone reading", "the replies"), so "you" means the reader, not the account holder. Keep that part; never open with "Are you sure about that?", which reads as aimed at them.
- Never say or imply the account holder is wrong, bluffing or lying, and never add a line about them. R8 never goes under a post by anyone from NVIDIA or Amazon.
- Skip posts about accidents, layoffs, harm or anything somber.
- Post by hand from your own account: no scheduler, no bots, no second accounts.
- If someone disputes an answer, reply with the source; if they are right, retire the template.
- R4, R7, R12 and Q3 use the "90% sure, right about 70% of the time" line. The repo has no citation for it yet (show-hn.md uses the same line); pin one before using them.
