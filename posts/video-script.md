# 25-second vertical video: "Prove it"

Format: 9:16, 1080×1920 (the intro clip is 720×1280; scale it up), 25 s, captions burned in, works with the sound off.
One text line on screen at a time (the end card has two), in the top third so it never covers the game; white text with a soft dark shadow;
cut each line on the beat. Every line is at most 6 words (the URL counts as one).

## Before you record

- Record a real quick round in a phone-sized window (375 px wide) with sound on.
- On one question, pick the answer you think is wrong and stake **100%**. That is what makes the screen show the
  **BLUFF** stamp on your pick, "✗ Wrong at 100% sure", **−300** and a reaction line, and it is what earns the
  end-screen roast (the roast needs a miss at 70% or more). Nothing on screen is faked; it is a real miss on purpose.
- Finish the round, then jump-cut to the end screen: score, type, the grid of squares, the roast line.
