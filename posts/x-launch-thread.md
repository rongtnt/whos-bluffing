# X launch thread (post Tue Oct 20, morning US Eastern, with the Show HN)

There is no project X account yet (`X_URL` in `web/public/site.js` is empty), so this is written to post from your
personal account. Attach `web/public/press/screen-discord.png` to tweet 5 and `web/public/press/screen-question.png`
to tweet 1. Rewrite in your own words; keep the facts. Every tweet is under 280 characters.

**1/**
Which is longer: the Nile or the Danube?

Easy. Now the real question: how sure are you? 60%? 90%? 100%?

I built a small game that scores your confidence, not just your answer. It's called Who's Bluffing? 🧵

**2/**
The scoring is simple. Say 50% and you get 0 points, right or wrong. Say 100% and you get +100 if you're right and −300
if you're wrong.

So being sure only pays when you are right. Bluffing costs you.

**3/**
Most of us are more sure than right. The game shows you by how much: every round ends with your type (Bluffer,
Hot-headed, Calibrated, Modest or Hedger) and a link to challenge a friend on the same ten questions.

**4/**
Every answer comes with its source, so you can check it. Spot a wrong one? Flag it and it gets reviewed.

**5/**
It also lives in Discord. Add the bot and it posts one question a day in your channel. Everyone answers privately.
Eight hours later the post turns into the reveal: the answer, how the server split, the top 5, and the bluff of the day.

**6/**
Roast mode is off by default, so the bluffer stays anonymous. Turn it on and the reveal names them.

There's a Slack app too, for a team's daily question.

**7/**
Free. No ads, no accounts. Source published on GitHub. The anonymous answers feed a study of overconfidence I
registered before launch; the pooled data will be released.
