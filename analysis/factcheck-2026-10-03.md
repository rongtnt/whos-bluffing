### Method and limits

- Wikipedia infoboxes were read as raw wikitext through the MediaWiki API (the cited article URL is the page that was read). Values that an infobox pulls from Wikidata would not count as independent; none of the cited fields did.
- britannica.com, rsc.org, ox.ac.uk, whc.unesco.org, teara.govt.nz, mayoclinic.org and toureiffel.paris return HTTP 403 (bot protection) to automated fetches. Those pages were read from their latest Internet Archive snapshot, and the table cites the timestamped archive URL. No bot check or CAPTCHA was bypassed. NCBI Bookshelf (StatPearls) showed a reCAPTCHA and was not opened.
- Offline references listed as sources (CRC Handbook, Gray's Anatomy, Feller) were replaced by an equivalent fetchable source or a computation; arithmetic items were recomputed in Python.
- WRONG requires two independent sources that agree; one disagreeing source alone gives CHECK.
