About Who's Bluffing

Who's Bluffing? is a free daily game about how sure you are. Each round asks ten either-or questions, such as “Which is longer: the Nile or the Danube?”; players pick an answer and stake how sure they are, from 50% to 100%, and a proper scoring rule makes honest confidence the winning strategy. Every answer comes with its source. There is one ranked round a day, the same for everyone, unlimited quick rounds by pack and difficulty, a daily question for Slack and Discord, and a classroom mode. The anonymous answers feed a pre-registered study of overconfidence whose data will be released openly. Who's Bluffing is independent and non-commercial, with no accounts and no ads.
